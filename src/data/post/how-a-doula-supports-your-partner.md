A doula does not take your partner’s place. I support the family as a whole, with your preferences and consent at the center. Before labor, we can talk together about what helps you feel cared for, what your partner hopes to do, and what kinds of help feel comfortable to you.

## Shared preparation

Partners often have their own questions about labor and how they can be useful. We can discuss practical ways to participate, such as offering reassurance, helping with comfort measures, or taking care of small tasks. You can also talk about how you would like support offered and what to do if your preferences change.

## Support during labor

Your partner remains an important part of your support team. I can suggest a comfort measure, help your partner find a position that feels sustainable, or offer reassurance when the room feels unfamiliar. I can also give your partner a moment to rest or step out while continuing to support you.

Every family is different. You decide how you want your partner and doula involved, and your clinical team remains responsible for medical care.
