An overnight postpartum visit can offer practical help with your newborn and a chance to rest, if that is the kind of support your family wants. Postpartum care can be arranged on its own; you do not need to have hired a birth doula first.

## What might happen during a visit?

Support is shaped around your priorities. It may include practical newborn care, parent education, feeding education within the doula’s non-clinical role, and time for you to rest while your baby is cared for. You can discuss what help would be most useful before care begins.

Doula and newborn-care support is not medical care. It does not replace your baby’s pediatric clinician or your own healthcare team. Questions about symptoms, feeding concerns that need clinical assessment, or other health needs should go to a qualified healthcare professional.
