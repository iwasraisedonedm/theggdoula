Postpartum support is available on its own. You do not need to have hired a birth doula first. Some families want help with practical newborn care, while others value education, time to rest, or an extra pair of hands as they settle in at home.

## Support shaped around your family

Postpartum care can include practical newborn care, parent education, feeding education within a non-clinical scope, and help with everyday tasks. The visit is shaped around what you want help with. The doula does not diagnose, provide medical treatment, or replace your family’s healthcare providers.

Before care begins, you can discuss the kind of support you are looking for, your routines and preferences, and the current visit arrangements. Service details and availability are confirmed directly so you can decide with clear information.

## Birth and postpartum care are separate choices

You may choose birth support, postpartum care, or both. The right starting point depends on your family’s circumstances and the kind of support you want. There is no single path that every family needs to follow.
