This page explains the scope of the Grit + Grace Doula website and its external links. It is not a service agreement; any doula services are discussed and arranged directly with Teesee.

## Website information

This website shares general information about doula services and educational resources. It is not medical advice or clinical care. For questions about your health or your baby’s health, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent or emergency needs, contact the appropriate local emergency or clinical service.

Service descriptions and resource information are provided to help visitors learn about available support. They do not confirm availability or reserve services. A consultation is scheduled only after Cal.com confirms the booking.

## Forms and external services

Use the Contact form for general inquiries, not emergencies. Please do not send sensitive health information through the form.

Scheduling is provided by Cal.com, and Birth Better Guide checkout and delivery are provided by Squarespace. Those services open on their own websites and are subject to their terms and privacy notices. Grit + Grace Doula does not process booking or store payments on this website.
