_Last updated_: October 26, 2026

This notice describes the information handled through the Grit + Grace Doula website and its linked services. It reflects the site’s current forms, scheduling links, store links, and analytics configuration.

## Information you submit

The contact form asks for your name, email address, and message. When you submit it, the form sends those details through [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/) to Grit + Grace Doula so Teesee can respond. The form is for general inquiries, not urgent requests or sensitive health information. Please do not include medical details.

You can also contact Teesee using the email address or phone link on the Contact page. Those communications use the email, phone, or messaging services you choose; they are not submitted through this website’s contact form.

## Scheduling and purchases

The Book page links to [Cal.com](https://cal.com/privacy) to choose a consultation time and enter booking details. Booking information is submitted to Cal.com. Cal.com handles scheduling, confirmation, cancellation, and rescheduling; this site does not receive or confirm the booking details.

The Birth Better Guide page links to the [Grit + Grace Squarespace store](https://falcon-dragonfly-kdrc.squarespace.com/store/p/birth-better-resource-guide). Checkout and PDF delivery happen there. Order and payment information is entered on Squarespace, not on this website.

Cal.com and Squarespace have their own privacy notices and practices. Review those notices before submitting information on their sites.

## Site analytics and browser storage

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure page views and Vercel Speed Insights to measure site performance. Google Analytics is not configured. See [Vercel’s Web Analytics privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) and [Speed Insights privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/privacy-policy) for details about their collection and processing.

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. It does not create a user account or mailing-list subscription.

## Third-party services

When you follow a link to Formspree, Cal.com, Squarespace, or another external site, that provider’s privacy practices apply to the information you submit there. This site does not control their data handling. Their policies may change, so review the current notice on the provider’s own site.
