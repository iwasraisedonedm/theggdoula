## Design Context

### Users

Pregnant and postpartum families in Orange County exploring nonmedical support for pregnancy, birth, feeding, postpartum recovery, and newborn care. They need clear, trustworthy explanations of what each service can offer and a straightforward way to ask questions or book a consultation.

### Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, and respectful. The experience should feel calm and personal, support each family's agency, and avoid pressure or judgment.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a calm editorial language: warm paper, botanical olive, deep midnight surfaces, generous whitespace, clear type, and fine structural rules. Support both light and dark themes. The homepage is a complete consultation-focused landing page with a deep olive hero, tiny animated letters spelling “teesee | the gg doula,” verified service options, a direct path to the complimentary 30-minute consultation, and authentic care imagery. Use Inter for reading and interface text, Newsreader for approved editorial display roles, and restrained monospace labels. Choose authentic imagery related to the people and care being described.

Avoid stock technology imagery, invented testimonials, unsupported health or outcome claims, arbitrary colors, decorative shadows, gradients, rounded ordinary UI, and mechanically repeated section treatments. Keep earth tones decorative rather than competing with brand actions.

### Design Principles

1. Put clear service information and useful next steps ahead of decoration.
2. Respect family choice; describe doula care accurately as nonmedical support alongside a clinical care team.
3. Prefer shared semantic tokens, typography roles, and components over page-specific styling.
4. Preserve the editorial page rhythm without copying the homepage layout onto every route.
5. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion, responsive, and touch-target expectations in both themes.
