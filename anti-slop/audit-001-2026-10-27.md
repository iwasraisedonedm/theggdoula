# Anti-slop audit 001 — 2026-10-27

**Scope:** Homepage (`src/pages/index.astro`) against the requested first phase of a full landing page redesign.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH — Replace the current full homepage with the requested opening screen (R-37)

The current homepage opens with a descriptive headline, consultation and services links, and a portrait, then continues through the complete trust, services, FAQ, and contact sections. The new direction is an olive-background opening with a restrained animated identity reading “teesee | the gg doula,” with the rest of the landing page to be designed later. The current page therefore does not yet express the newly supplied direction or the requested first phase.

**Suggested fix:** Make the first phase a single full-viewport introduction. Use the supplied animation as a reference for gentle letter-based motion, adapted to the doula identity; keep the olive field and wordmark central. Preserve a still, reduced-motion presentation and keep the brand name available to assistive technology. Resume the rest of the homepage after this opening concept is approved.
