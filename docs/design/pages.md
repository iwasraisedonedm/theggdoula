# Information architecture and page plan

This page records the approved route and content architecture for Grit + Grace Doula. It is a planning document: the route inventory below describes the current source, while the target routes and content specifications describe the intended information architecture. No page copy, service claims, credentials, pricing, or testimonials are approved by this plan unless independently verified.

## Current implementation audit

The Services overview and its five data-backed detail routes have since been implemented in `src/pages/services.astro`, `src/pages/services/[service].astro`, and `src/data/services.ts`; prenatal, birth, breastfeeding education, and newborn care now use page-specific compositions in `src/components/services/PrenatalDetail.astro`, `src/components/services/BirthDetail.astro`, `src/components/services/BreastfeedingDetail.astro`, and `src/components/services/NewbornCareDetail.astro`. The feeding and newborn-care pages distinguish nonmedical education and practical support from clinical assessment, and direct clinical concerns to licensed professionals. The About page is implemented in `src/pages/about.astro` using copied biography, approach, credentials, and local copies of authentic image assets from the separate `~/Projects/doula` reference checkout. Any additional biography details, including Teesee's specific motivation for doula work, are reserved for the final content refinement pass. Transfers stay self-contained: links use only routes in this checkout, and no page links to the reference project. The `/book/` route links to the verified Cal.com event, and header, footer, home, services overview, About, and service-page consultation CTAs lead there. Cal.com owns the booking, confirmation, cancellation, and rescheduling flow; this site has no custom booking result routes. Google Analytics is not configured (`id: null`), while the existing Vercel Analytics integration remains enabled. The Contact route now uses the configured Formspree endpoint for four required fields, with a honeypot and client-side status handling. It also provides a direct email/phone fallback and uses a brief purpose-specific privacy notice. The site-wide privacy policy remains a template and needs its own content review. The Resources hub is implemented in `src/pages/resources/index.astro`; its link is in the primary navigation and footer, while the existing Blog route remains linked in the footer. The hub links to live service routes, uses the verified Birth Better Guide description, and links to the FAQ anchor on the homepage. The current collection contains seven AstroWind/template posts and no doula-specific articles; the hub filters out unrelated posts instead of featuring them. No guide download asset is present in this checkout, so printable downloads remain a content gap for the final refinement pass.

### Content and link findings

- `src/content.config.ts` and `src/data/post/` are the active `post` collection. Keep this collection and its schema; do not replace it with a new CMS or hand-coded blog list.
- The current post permalink pattern `/%slug%` puts posts at root. Move it under the resource blog route as `/resources/blog/%slug%`; move the listing to `/resources/blog/`. Decide taxonomy URL policy as part of that migration (preferred: keep category/tag archives under `/resources/blog/category/` and `/resources/blog/tag/`, or disable them if they do not serve visitors).
- Existing article slugs include `astrowind-template-in-depth`, `build-websites-with-ai`, `get-started-website-with-astro-tailwind-css`, `how-to-customize-astrowind-to-your-brand`, `landing`, `markdown-elements-demo-post`, and `useful-resources-to-create-websites`. Audit these for relevance and approval before republishing. Preserve source files and metadata during review; do not present template documentation as doula guidance.
- Root-level post URLs can collide with future top-level routes. A migration must map every published old URL to its new resource URL with a permanent redirect, and update internal links, canonicals, sitemap, RSS, related-post links, and any external references under our control. Keep redirects for retired demo URLs only where a meaningful destination exists; do not redirect unrelated content to the homepage.
- `src/navigation.ts` currently exposes Services, an in-page “Our approach” link, About, and Blog, plus a consultation action. Footer groups expose Services, approach, About, Contact, Blog, Terms, and Privacy. Replace the in-page approach item with the Resource hub, retain the legal links, and ensure every configured URL resolves.
- Homepage service cards point to prenatal, birth, postpartum, and breastfeeding routes. The first three and breastfeeding currently 404; there is no separate newborn-care card/route. Split breastfeeding and newborn care into separate service entries, each linking to its intended page.
- Homepage FAQ content is only a three-question teaser. The target FAQ route does not exist. Use the teaser only as a short preview linking to `/resources/faq/`; the full FAQ must be fact-checked and answer real client questions.
- Header, footer, homepage, About, services overview, and service-detail consultation CTAs lead to the verified `/book/` route and Cal.com event. Contact is the separate general inquiry route and uses Formspree.
- `src/pages/contact.astro` has generic sample details and copy; `src/components/ui/Form.astro` renders a form shell, but no submission endpoint/integration was located. Confirm the actual form recipient/handling and privacy language before relying on it.
- Sample testimonials and stock business/team information appear in template routes, including `src/pages/services.astro`, `src/pages/about.astro`, `src/pages/homes/*`, and `src/pages/landing/*`. None qualify as authentic approved testimonials. Do not create `/testimonials/` until permission and attribution/redaction terms are confirmed; do not include sample testimonial widgets in production pages.

## Approved hierarchy and navigation

### Primary navigation

1. Services — `/services/`
2. About — `/about/`
3. Resources — `/resources/`
4. Contact — `/contact/`
5. Persistent primary action: **Book a free 30-minute consultation** — `/book/`

Keep service detail routes one level below Services. The Resources page links to Blog, Guides, Better Birth Guide, and FAQ. Do not put every resource or service detail in the header. Do not retain a separate “Our approach” navigation item: explain the approach within About and link to the relevant section from the home page if useful.

### Footer hierarchy

- **Services:** overview and the five service detail pages.
- **Resources:** resource hub, blog, guides, Better Birth Guide, FAQ.
- **Grit + Grace:** About, Contact, and Testimonials only when approved testimonials exist.
- **Legal:** Privacy and Terms.
- Keep the RSS link as a feed utility. Keep the booking action visually and verbally consistent with the header; do not add a second conversion objective.

## Conversion journeys

- **Service discovery:** Home → Services → relevant service detail → `/book/`.
- **Direct service search:** Service detail → related service/FAQ as needed → `/book/`.
- **Provider fit:** Home or service detail → About → Services/FAQ → `/book/`.
- **Learning first:** Home or search → Resources → Blog/Guide/FAQ → relevant service → `/book/`.
- **Question before commitment:** `/contact/` handles general inquiries with its Formspree form and direct email/phone alternatives; `/book/` remains the route for scheduling.
- **Booking intent:** consultation CTAs → `/book/` → verified Cal.com consultation event; Cal.com handles booking confirmation and appointment management.

Cal.com owns the confirmation and booking management experience; this project has no custom booking success, cancellation, or error routes. ## Search and link acceptance criteria

- Every route linked in the header, footer, home page, or a service/resource page resolves to a page with its intended content.
- `/book/` resolves to a functioning scheduling path and receives consistent links from every page template.
- No root-level blog post slug conflicts with a canonical page route after migration; all former public post URLs have one permanent destination.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap entries, RSS items, breadcrumbs, related-post links, taxonomy links, and pagination use the chosen resource hierarchy and slash policy.
- No indexable page contains demo/placeholder copy, unsupported service or credential claims, sample contact details, or unapproved testimonials.
- Each route has a distinct title/description aligned with the content actually published; do not use structured FAQ data for answers that are not visible on the page.
