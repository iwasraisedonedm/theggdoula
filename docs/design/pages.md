# Information architecture and page plan

This page records the approved route and content architecture for Grit + Grace Doula. The route inventory describes the current source; the specifications describe intended page purposes and conversion paths. Repository content and configured integrations are the source of truth for published claims.

## Current implementation audit

The Services overview and its five data-backed detail routes are implemented in `src/pages/services.astro`, `src/pages/services/[service].astro`, and `src/data/services.ts`; prenatal, birth, breastfeeding education, and newborn care use page-specific compositions in `src/components/services/`. The About page uses approved source content and authentic image assets. Links stay within this checkout except for the configured Cal.com and Squarespace services. The `/book/` route links to the Cal.com event; Cal.com owns appointment confirmation and changes, and this static site has no local booking result routes. Google Analytics is unconfigured (`id: null`); Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights are enabled in the shared layout, with no custom CTA event tracking. Contact uses the configured Formspree endpoint, requires name, email, and message, includes a honeypot, and reports client-side submission status. Privacy and Terms describe the current site data flows. The Resources hub, six local PDF guides, Better Birth Guide store link, canonical blog index/articles, and approved client-experience page are implemented. The FAQ content remains on the homepage and relevant service pages; there is no standalone `/resources/faq/` route.

- `src/navigation.ts` exposes Services, an in-page “Our approach” link, About, and Resources, plus a consultation action. The footer retains direct Blog, Resources, and utility links; Blog now resolves under `/resources/blog/`.

- FAQ disclosures remain on relevant service detail pages; there is no standalone `/resources/faq/` route. The homepage FAQ section will be reconsidered as the landing page is rebuilt.
- Header, footer, About, services overview, and service-detail consultation CTAs lead to the verified `/book/` route and Cal.com event. Contact is the separate general inquiry route and uses Formspree.

## Approved hierarchy and navigation

### Primary navigation

1. Services — `/services/`
2. About — `/about/`
3. Resources — `/resources/`
4. Contact — `/contact/`
5. Persistent primary action: **Book a free 30-minute consultation** — `/book/`

Keep service detail routes one level below Services. The Resources page links to Blog, Guides, Better Birth Guide, and FAQ. Do not put every resource or service detail in the header. Do not retain a separate “Our approach” navigation item: explain the approach within About and link to the relevant section from the home page if useful.

### Footer hierarchy

- **Services:** overview and the five service detail pages.
- **Resources:** resource hub, blog, guides, Better Birth Guide, FAQ.
- **Grit + Grace:** About, Contact, and Testimonials only when approved testimonials exist.
- **Legal:** Privacy and Terms.
- Keep the RSS link as a feed utility. Keep the booking action visually and verbally consistent with the header; do not add a second conversion objective.

## Page specifications and conversion paths

Every indexable page needs a unique, accurate title and description, one clear page purpose, and internal links that support the next likely question. The free 30-minute consultation is the primary conversion across the site. Use the same `/book/` destination and label consistently. The page-specific CTA below means one prominent action plus contextual text links where useful, not repeated competing buttons.

## Conversion journeys

- **Service discovery:** Home → Services → relevant service detail → `/book/`.
- **Direct service search:** Service detail → related service/FAQ as needed → `/book/`.
- **Provider fit:** Home or service detail → About → Services/FAQ → `/book/`.
- **Learning first:** Home or search → Resources → Blog/Guide/FAQ → relevant service → `/book/`.
- **Question before commitment:** `/contact/` handles general inquiries with its Formspree form and direct email/phone alternatives; `/book/` remains the route for scheduling.
- **Booking intent:** consultation CTAs → `/book/` → verified Cal.com consultation event; Cal.com handles booking confirmation and appointment management.

## Audit findings and remaining notes

1. `src/config/site.ts` keeps `trailingSlash: false`; Astro canonicals and `getPermalink()` use no trailing slash, while Vercel serves the established slash-form links. Keep migration redirects aligned with this policy.
2. The blog migration is implemented: canonical listing and article routes are under `/resources/blog/`; legacy list and taxonomy URLs redirect. Taxonomy archives, pagination, RSS, sitemap, canonicals, breadcrumbs, and related-post links use the new route pattern. Sitemap output omits tag archives and paginated pages that are marked noindex. Retired template-post URLs no longer have content routes.
3. Seven Teesee Logan articles are published in the existing collection with clinical scope stated, service pricing removed, and supplied references retained as links. sample posts have been removed.
4. The five service detail routes use `src/data/services.ts` and the compositions in `src/components/services/`. Keep service descriptions aligned with approved project content; do not add specific inclusions or claims without review. Add resource routes only when their content is ready. The homepage currently groups breastfeeding education and newborn-care information in one summary card while each has a separate detail route.
5. `/book/` links to the confirmed Cal.com event and direct email/phone fallback. Cal.com owns booking confirmation and changes; this static site has no local booking result routes. Google Analytics is unconfigured; Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights run through the shared layout.
6. About uses approved source content and verified qualifications. Contact uses the configured Formspree endpoint and current contact details. Privacy and Terms document actual site behavior. Services now has doula-specific overview content.
7. `/testimonials/` contains excerpts that the page labels as permission-backed. The route contains permission-backed client experiences; keep only approved excerpts and attribution.
8. The homepage's FAQ CTA points to the existing `#common-questions` section. The planned standalone `/resources/faq/` route is still absent; add it only when the page is requested, using approved answers already on the site.
9. Demonstration pages and template-only draft articles were removed from source. The production build should contain no `/homes/`, `/landing/`, or `/pricing/` routes, and no template-only root-level articles.
10. The contact form's success/error state logic has been exercised with mocked provider responses. No real Formspree inquiry was submitted during the full-site audit, and Cal.com availability/confirmation was not tested; those provider-owned transactions remain external verification items.

## Search and link acceptance criteria

- Every route linked in the header, footer, home page, or a service/resource page resolves to a page with its intended content.
- `/book/` resolves to a functioning scheduling path and receives consistent links from every page template.
- No root-level blog post slug conflicts with a canonical page route after migration; all former public post URLs have one permanent destination.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap entries, RSS items, breadcrumbs, related-post links, taxonomy links, and pagination use the chosen resource hierarchy and slash policy.
- No indexable page contains demo/placeholder copy, unsupported service or credential claims, sample contact details, or unapproved testimonials.
- Each route has a distinct title/description aligned with the content actually published; do not use structured FAQ data for answers that are not visible on the page.
