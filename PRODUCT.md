# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pregnant and postpartum families in Orange County seeking doula support and reliable information about care options.

## Product Purpose

Grit + Grace Doula explains pregnancy, birth, postpartum, breastfeeding, and newborn-care support, and gives families ways to learn more or start a conversation. Success is a family understanding the available support and choosing whether to inquire or book a consultation.

## Positioning

The site presents personalized, nonjudgmental doula care centered on each family's voice and choices. No unique service mechanism or comparative claim is established in the current source material; do not invent one.

## Operating Context

Visitors read service information and educational resources, review common questions, use a postpartum planning estimate, and follow contact or consultation links. The site serves families across Orange County, California.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Published service information covers prenatal support, birth doula support, postpartum care, breastfeeding education, and newborn care.
- The postpartum planner estimates support hours from visitor-selected weeks and hours per week. It is illustrative, not a quote, package, or availability confirmation.
- Preserve the existing routes, navigation, service records, assets, and contact/booking integrations.
- Do not imply clinical care, guaranteed outcomes, a quote, or current availability unless supported by approved source material.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Grit + Grace Doula name, the warm and respectful voice, and the current service identity. The homepage is being redesigned in stages, beginning with the olive, animated “teesee | the gg doula” hero. Keep later sections accurate to the existing services and retain the site's framework, routes, and integrations.

## Evidence on Hand

- Service definitions and routes: `src/data/services.ts` and `src/pages/services/`.
- Current homepage hero: `src/pages/index.astro`. Future landing-page content should use the verified service, navigation, and integration sources listed above.
- Brand and design constraints: `docs/design/brand.md` and `docs/design/system.md`.
- Use only approved client experiences and other real evidence already in the project. Do not fabricate testimonials, endorsements, prices, outcomes, credentials, or availability.

## Product Principles

- Respect each family's voice, values, and choices.
- Make service options and next steps clear and easy to find.
- Describe support without pressure or unsupported promises.
